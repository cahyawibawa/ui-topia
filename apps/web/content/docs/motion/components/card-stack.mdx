## How it works

The deck is one container with every card absolutely centered in it. Hovering the container flips a single `open` flag, and each card derives its own rotation and offset from its distance to the middle of the deck. Nothing is keyed per card, so changing **Cards** or **Spread** reflows the whole fan.

Three states drive everything:

- **Open:** the container is hovered, so the fan widens.
- **Hovered:** the card under the pointer lifts a little and takes the top of the stack.
- **Active:** a clicked card scales up into the middle while the others step aside and dim. Clicking away or pressing `Escape` sends it back.

Motion animates `x`, `y`, `rotate`, `scale` and `opacity` with one spring. **Bounce** is the only physics knob: at 0 the cards settle, and past about 5 they wobble. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the transition is instant.

**Scatter** adds a fixed pseudo-random offset per card, derived from its index. It looks hand-dealt, but it never changes between renders, so there is no flicker.

## Usage

```tsx
import CardStack from "@/components/ui/card-stack";

<CardStack bounce={0.3} corner={14} scatter={0.2} spread={60}>
  <img alt="" src="/one.jpg" />
  <img alt="" src="/two.jpg" />
  <img alt="" src="/three.jpg" />
</CardStack>;
```

Each child fills its card, so pass anything: images, gradients or a small layout.
