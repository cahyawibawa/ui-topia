Three stamps rest in a fanned row on a dark table. Reach for one and it lifts. Press it and it comes to the front while the others settle behind. With a stamp in front, move the pointer: the stamp leans toward it, and the print slides under its own window. Press the stamp again, press the table, or hit `Escape` to put them back in the row.

## How it works

- **The paper is one SVG path.** The perforation is bitten out of the edge of the path, so the shadow follows the scallops instead of the stamp's bounding box. A mask over a rectangle would shadow the box.
- **The lift is a tween, not a spring.** The stamp rises 14px. A spring covers about a pixel and a half a frame there and spends most of its time creeping the last pixel, which reads as lag. Everything bigger, like coming forward and settling back, is a spring.
- **Two springs, two speeds.** The lean and the print follow the same pointer through springs of different stiffness, so the print trails the stamp instead of riding it.
- **Reduced motion** skips the lean and the parallax and makes every move instant.

## Usage

```tsx
import StampCollection from "@/components/ui/stamp-collection";

<StampCollection />;
```

The collection draws its own stage and fills the width of its container, at a fixed height of 25rem. The stamps and their prints are the `STAMPS` array at the top of the file, and the colours are the constants above it, so restyle it there.
