---
title: Optical Alignment
description: Align to what looks centered, not what measures centered.
order: 2
status: soon
---

Geometric centering is a starting point. Some shapes carry visual weight to one side, so measured alignment looks off.

A play triangle in a circle needs a nudge toward its pointed side. An icon next to text often wants a pixel or two of offset so the glyph and the label share a baseline.

```tsx
<span className="translate-x-px">
  <PlayIcon />
</span>
```

Trust your eye over the inspector. Make the adjustment on the asset itself when you can, so every use gets it for free.
