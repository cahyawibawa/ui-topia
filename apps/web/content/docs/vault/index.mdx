A collection of design engineering notes. They are short and simple, meant as a pile of useful ideas and tricks rather than an exhaustive resource.

Many of these ideas are well known in the craft community. The format of this section is inspired by [Craft](https://craft.gustavofior.com/) by Gustavo Fior, which is worth reading in full.

Component reference lives under **Base** and **Motion**. This section is for the thinking behind it.
